# Octank China — Employee Handbook (HR, 2026 edition)

This handbook applies to all Octank employees in mainland China. It is a synthetic test
fixture for the Launchpad-CN end-to-end scenario suite; it describes no real company.

## Working hours and HR help desk

- Standard working hours are 09:00–18:00 China Standard Time, Monday to Friday.
- The HR help desk answers tickets within 2 business days.
- The HR hotline is staffed 09:00–17:30 CST on working days.

## Annual leave

- Employees with fewer than 5 years of service accrue **15 days** of annual leave per year.
- Employees with 5 or more years of service accrue **20 days** of annual leave per year.
- Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next calendar year; the rest lapses on 31 March.
- Leave requests must be approved by the employee's line manager in the HR portal.

## Probation

- The probation period is **90 days** for individual contributors and **180 days** for people managers.
- A probation review meeting is held in the last two weeks of the period.

## Public holidays

- Octank observes the statutory PRC public holidays published by the State Council each year.
- Work on a statutory holiday is compensated at 300 % of the daily rate.

## Payroll

- Salaries are paid on the **25th** of each month; when the 25th falls on a weekend or public
  holiday, payment is made on the preceding working day.
- Payslips are available in the HR portal on the payment day.
- Questions about net pay, pension and tax are handled by the payroll specialist.
