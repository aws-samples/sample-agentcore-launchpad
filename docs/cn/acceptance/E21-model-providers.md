# E21 Model Providers 「模型提供方」 (section `model-providers`, `/v2/settings/model-providers`, D21) — acceptance standard

**Risk score:** 10 (R4 Global Bedrock API-key leakage: SSM names, Identity provider names and journal rows must never carry key material; R9 admin-only surface with 13 routes; cross-partition probes) → negatives ≥ 6 (this sheet lists 15) · **Recorded build:** to fill (host `GET /api/health`) · **Status:** draft

## 1. Scope and roles
- **Routes (`backend/app/core/route_policy.py`, all `ADMIN`, all `WORKSPACE_EXEMPT`):** `GET/POST /api/settings/model-providers`, `GET …/changes`, `PUT …/workspaces/{workspace_id}/models`, `GET/PUT/DELETE …/{provider_id}`, `POST …/{provider_id}/credential`, `POST …/{provider_id}/probe`, `POST …/{provider_id}/models`, `PUT/DELETE …/{provider_id}/models/{model_ref}`, `POST …/models/{model_ref}/probe`. No member-grantable `models.manage` permission exists (user ruling). Observable downstream: `GET /api/capabilities` (`MEMBER`, workspace-scoped) `catalog_version`, `models[]`, `model_providers{}`, `embedding`.
- **Roles:** `demo-admin` only; `hr-intern` sees `v2-mp-forbidden` and gets 403 on every route.
- **Seed state (round 13, N13-obs7, `cn/r13-c`):** a **fresh** ledger seeds `bedrock` only (required, `configured true` on the hub) with `global.zai.glm-5.3` (default chat), `global.moonshotai.kimi-k3` and `amazon.titan-embed-text-v2:0` (default embedding) — three `builtin` rows, one `seed` journal row. The `default` host's ledger predates R13 and **still shows** the `siliconflow` row (builtin, optional, `configured false`) with four disabled chat rows; a new environment has no such row. An administrator adds SiliconFlow (or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint) with `POST /api/settings/model-providers` naming the SSM parameter of its key — the registry stores **names only** (spec C4).
- **Out of scope:** the embedding reindex guard beyond its 409 (M1-d / s12f, E17); per-agent model selection (E03); the key material itself (Q2b scan covers leakage).

TEST: asserting the pre-R13 `siliconflow` row on a fresh ledger or its absence on `default`; reading `catalog_version` after a credential or probe write and expecting a bump (audit-only rows never advance it, D21-obs5/6/7). PRODUCT: any key fragment in a response, journal row, log line or screenshot; any 200 on `PUT` with `name` / `kind`; any member 2xx.
