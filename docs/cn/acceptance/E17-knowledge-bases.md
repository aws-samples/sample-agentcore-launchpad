# E17 Knowledge Bases 「知识库」 (section `knowledge-bases`, `/v2/knowledge-bases`) — acceptance standard

**Risk score:** 12 (R2 upstream: self-built OpenSearch + S3 + SQS/Lambda chunker + Titan embeddings, WB-007 / WB-008 history; R9 isolation on `knowledge_bases.created_by`) → negatives ≥ 10 (this sheet lists 14) · **Recorded build:** to fill (host `GET /api/health`) · **Status:** draft

## 1. Scope and roles
- **Routes (`backend/app/core/route_policy.py`):** `GET/POST /api/knowledge-bases`, `GET/PATCH/DELETE …/{kb_id}`, `POST …/{kb_id}/files`, `POST …/{kb_id}/data-sources`, `DELETE …/{kb_id}/data-sources/{ds_id}`, `GET …/data-sources/{ds_id}/documents`, `GET …/data-sources/{ds_id}/ingestion-jobs`, `POST …/data-sources/{ds_id}/sync`, `POST …/{kb_id}/query` (retrieval playground) = `MEMBER`, all workspace-scoped. Backend = `services/knowledge_selfhosted.py` behind the `KnowledgeBackend` Protocol (503 `feature.unavailable` until registered).
- **Roles:** `demo-admin` owns `hr-policy-kb` (3 documents) and the temporary KB of chapter ⑨; `hr-intern` queries and reads. `created_by` is stamped server-side on `knowledge_bases` (`users-and-ownership.md` §1); under `isolated` a member sees only their own / NULL-creator / admin-created KBs, and a hidden `kb_id` answers `404 kb.not_found` **before** any OpenSearch or S3 call. `attached_agents` drops hidden agent names while `attached_agent_count` keeps the true number.
- **Out of scope:** the `hr-policy-reader` agent's deployment (E02) and its Chat quality beyond "answers cite the KB" (E04); the Titan embedding model row and the reindex guard (E21 / s12f); memory (E06).

## 6. Failure classification
Per `tests/e2e/README.md` §Failure classification. Module-specific ENV signals: OpenSearch 5xx / `ReadTimeout` (surfaced as `503 kb.backend_unavailable` or `jobs_degraded true` — product correct), SQS → Lambda chunker lag pushing the ingestion past 15 min, Titan embedding `ThrottlingException` via the Global Bedrock key (cross-partition), S3 `SlowDown`. TEST: polling the detail inside the jobs-cache TTL and reading a stale `STARTING`; asserting `cleanup.* == "ok"` strictly when the product answered `pending` + a job (that is WB-008 behaving). Everything else PRODUCT, in particular any 502 after a delete already committed, a FAILED KB without `failure_reasons`, or a 403 on a hidden KB.
